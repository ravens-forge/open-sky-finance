Open Sky Finance is a free and open-source personal finance app. Track your money in a clear, paper-ledger style, with no limits, no ads and no sign-up.

Everything stays on your device: the app has no internet access, no user accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

Features:
• Unlimited assets accounts: bank, cash, credit cards, loans, investments, crypto, property…
• Income, expenses and transfers with categories, subcategories and labels
• Configurable home page with cash flow, budget, net income and net worth charts
• Balance sheet, net income statement, label totals and calendar
• Monthly budgets per category
• Reminders for recurring bills and income, and scheduled transactions
• Trash to restore deleted transactions
• Backups in an open, documented JSON format
• Import from Bluecoins backups
• English, Spanish and French

Open Sky Finance is free and open source, released under the GPL-3.0 license.
